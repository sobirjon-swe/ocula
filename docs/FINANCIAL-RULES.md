# Sales and payroll rule decisions

Implementation reference for PROJECT.md §§7.6–7.8, 7.12, 7.19–7.21.

- `POST /api/v1/checkout` uses the order creation payload with `type: quick` and `payments: [{amount, method}]`. It creates the order, accepts split payments, then hands over goods in one database transaction. Stock shortage, invalid payment, or denied credit rolls back the entire checkout. Existing idempotency middleware covers the endpoint.
- Ordinary orders may be created with no prepayment. Credit is checked at handover. An identified customer and `sales.customer.debt.grant` are required when money remains due. The employee's individual `debt_limit` applies to this customer's existing delivered debt plus the new balance, across branches. A customer row lock serializes simultaneous credit sales.
- A director can approve an undelivered order with `POST /api/v1/orders/{id}/approve-debt` and a reason. The order stores director, timestamp, reason, and maximum approved customer exposure. Higher subsequent exposure requires new approval. Every handover uses the original sale creator's credit authority, including when a manager or driver executes it; collected field cash is recorded before handover within the delivery transaction.
- Payments and refunds belong to the currently open branch shift, rather than the original sale's shift. Payment remaining-balance validation and return quantity/refund validation execute while the order is locked.
- Revenue belongs to the branch shift open at handover. Updating the order's shift at handover preserves each advance payment's original shift. A shift crossing midnight keeps its opening business date; without a shift, recognition uses the Tashkent calendar date.
- Turnover uses recognized sales minus quantities returned in the report's own period; fully returned sales have zero net turnover. Returns from an older sale can produce negative net units in a later reporting period.
- Returns allocate order-level discounts and rounding across line values. Refunds cannot exceed either paid funds or the returned goods' discounted value. Full returns consume rounding residuals rather than accumulating per-unit rounding errors.
- Revenue and profit bonuses are calculated when goods are handed over and the sale is fully paid. Product-specific rules apply to matching lines; general rules cover other lines. Personal rules override role rules; branch rules override network rules. Calculated entries snapshot eligible item revenue, cost, and quantity so returns reverse the actual awarded basis.
- Count bonuses award one unit per completed master work order when its sale becomes fully paid, in that closure month's payroll period. `rate` is an explicit monetary amount per unit. Existing percentage-only rules retain their percentage arithmetic; no percentage is implicitly interpreted as a monetary rate.
- Master-error defects deduct one earned count unit each, capped at zero monthly net earnings. A defect consumes an earning once (`bonus_entries.defect_id` unique). Defects before an earning are retried at subsequent master accrual in the same month. Defects after payout create recoverable negative entries and preserve the original payout.
- Return adjustments are append only, cumulative, proportional, and idempotent, including paid bonuses. An unpaid bonus pays only its remaining amount after reversals. Paid negative adjustments remain visible in the bonus ledger for recovery; historical cash payouts are never rewritten.
- Reversing a payment reopens a closed sale and suspends its bonus entitlement. Repayment restores the surviving earned amount through linked adjustments; it does not create another original bonus or cash payout. Returned goods reduce the payable total, so a proper partial refund leaves the remaining paid goods debt free.
- `GET /api/v1/pos-config` exposes only rounding, discount-limit, and prepayment settings to employees allowed to create orders. Checkout applies the current stored director rounding setting.
- Cash reversals use the current open shift, with the original movement locked and duplicate reversal checked inside the same transaction. Historical closed-shift totals remain unchanged.
- Returns follow merged-variant chains to the active canonical stock identity and preserve the original sale item identity and historical FIFO cost.

- A suspended zero-entitlement bonus cannot be marked paid. Its payout request is deferred until entitlement is positive again, preserving the ability to pay after repayment.
- Cancellation locks and reloads the order before checking delivery and signed payments. Only unfulfilled orders with no retained payment can cancel; cancellation closes their collectible debt mirror and refreshes the customer balance.
- Reversing an uncollected field cash receipt locks the driver, reduces the held cash balance, and writes a signed payment reversal attributed to that driver. If any collection happened after the receipt, allocation is ambiguous and receipt reversal is refused; an audited cash refund/reconciliation is required instead.
